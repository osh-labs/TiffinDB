# Display settings

Choose **Display** in the top bar to open **Reading and display**. These settings are yours alone. They follow you to any device you sign in on, and they apply to every project and form. Other people's settings do not change yours, and yours do not change theirs.

## Colours

- Light (the standard)
- Dark
- Strong contrast, light
- Strong contrast, dark

## Accent colour

Teal (the standard), Indigo, Plum, or Graphite. The accent colour is used for buttons, links, and the sidebar. The two strong contrast colour choices do not use an accent colour.

## Text size

Small, Standard (the standard), Large, Larger, or Largest.

## Space between lines

Standard (the standard), Roomy, or Very roomy.

## Lettering and movement

The typeface used for TiffinDB's text:

- Atkinson Hyperlegible: made for easy reading.
- Lexend: wide and open letters.
- Inter (the standard): plain and compact.
- Source Serif: book-style letters with serifs.
- This device, sans-serif: the plain lettering your device uses.
- This device, serif: the book-style lettering your device uses.

The first four come with TiffinDB, so they look the same on every device. The last two use the lettering your device has, so they can look different from one device to another.

Tick "Reduce movement and animation" to turn off movement on the page. TiffinDB also turns movement off when your device's own settings ask for reduced motion.

## Saving and going back

- Choose **Save settings** to keep your choices. The notice says your display settings have been saved.
- Choose **Back to standard** to put every setting back to the standard choice.

The preview at the bottom of the page shows how a record will look, using a made-up record called Stand mixer. With JavaScript on, the preview changes as you choose; without it, it shows your saved settings until you save.
