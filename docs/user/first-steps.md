# First steps

## Signing in

Open the address of your TiffinDB in a web browser. The **Sign in** page asks for your username and password. Type them and choose **Sign in**.

- If the username and password do not match, the page says so and you can try again. After too many wrong tries, the page asks you to wait for a while before you try again.
- Tick "Show password" to see what you have typed, so you can check it.
- If you have forgotten your password, ask the person who looks after your TiffinDB. An Admin can give you a temporary password (see [For Admins](for-admins.md#people)).

### The first time you sign in

When an Admin adds you, they give you a username and a temporary password. The first time you sign in with it, TiffinDB asks you to choose your own password before you go on. Type the temporary password as your current password, then your new one twice, and choose **Change password**.

A new password needs at least 10 characters. A few unrelated words make a good password.

### Changing your password later

Your name is shown at the right of the top bar. Choose it to open **Change your password**. Type your current password, then the new one twice, and choose **Change password**.

## Your projects

After you sign in, you see **Your projects**. A project is one database: its tables, forms, and files. Each project shows how many tables and records it has, when it last changed, and who it is shared with.

- Choose a project's name, or **Open**, to go into it.
- Members see only the projects an Admin has shared with them. If you see none, ask an Admin to share one with you.
- Admins see every project, and also **New project**, **Import a project archive**, and **Sharing** (see [For Admins](for-admins.md)).

## Inside a project

Opening a project takes you to the list of records in its first table. If the project has no tables yet, it takes you to its tables instead.

### The sidebar

The sidebar on the left belongs to the project you are in. From top to bottom:

- The project's name, and **Switch project**, which takes you back to your projects.
- **Tables**: one link for each table, with the number of records in it. Choose a table to see its records.
- **Collections**: your saved searches, each with the number of records it holds, and **New collection** (see [Collections](collections.md)).
- **Data** (Admins only): **Import a file** and **Export** (see [Import and export](import-and-export.md)).
- **Tables and fields**: what the project's tables hold. Admins change them here; Members can look.
- **In the trash**, with the number of records in the trash (see [The trash](trash.md)).

### The top bar

The top bar is on every page once you are signed in:

- **TiffinDB**, at the left, takes you to your projects.
- **Projects** also takes you to your projects.
- **People**, **Plugins**, and **Activity** are shown to Admins only (see [For Admins](for-admins.md)).
- **Display** opens your display settings (see [Display settings](display-settings.md)).
- Your name opens **Change your password**.
- **Sign out** signs you out.

Inside a project, the top bar also has a search box that looks through every table of the project at once (see [Lists and search](lists-and-search.md#searching-all-tables)).

### The bottom of the page

The bottom of each page says that TiffinDB is free software and has two links: **About TiffinDB**, and **Source code**, which goes to the place where anyone can get TiffinDB's source code.

## Signing out

Choose **Sign out** in the top bar. The page then says that you have signed out. Sign out when you finish on a computer that other people use.

## What Members and Admins can do

| | Member | Admin |
|---|---|---|
| See and change records in projects shared with them | Yes | Yes, in every project |
| Move records to the trash and restore them | Yes | Yes |
| Make, change, and delete collections | Yes | Yes |
| Empty the trash | No | Yes |
| Import and export | No | Yes |
| Change tables, fields, and forms | No | Yes |
| Add people, create and share projects, turn plugins on and off | No | Yes |
