# The trash

When you choose **Move to trash** on a record, the record is not deleted. It waits in the project's trash until someone restores it or an Admin empties the trash. While it is there it does not show in lists, collections, or searches, and links to it are not shown.

The sidebar line **In the trash** shows how many records the project's trash holds. Choose it to open **Trash**.

## What the trash page shows

The records in the trash are grouped by table. Each one shows its title and when, and by whom, it was moved to the trash. When a table has many records in the trash, only the first few are shown; a link under them shows all of that table's records in the trash, and **Show every table** goes back to every table.

## Restoring records

- Choose **Restore** beside a record to bring it back.
- Choose **Restore all in Items** (with your own table's name) to bring back every record of that table that is in the trash.

A restored record comes back as it was, with its files and its links to other records. Members and Admins can both restore records.

Straight after you move a record to the trash, the notice at the top of the list also has an **Undo** button, which restores it at once.

## Emptying the trash (Admins)

Emptying the trash deletes every record in it for good, with the files that only those records hold.

1. On the trash page, choose **Empty the trash**.
2. The page **Empty the trash?** says how many records will be deleted, from which tables, and how many files.
3. Choose **Empty the trash** to delete them, or **Cancel** to keep them.

Before it empties the trash, TiffinDB saves a snapshot: a copy of the project as it was. An Admin can go back to that copy (see [For Admins](for-admins.md#snapshots)). Files are not part of snapshots, so the deleted files cannot be brought back that way.

If someone moved more records to the trash while you were looking at the confirmation, TiffinDB does not empty the trash. It shows the new numbers instead, and you can confirm again.
